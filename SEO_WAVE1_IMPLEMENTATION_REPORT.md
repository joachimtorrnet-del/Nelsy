## Intent Consolidation Decision

"booking app for nail techs", "nail tech booking software" and "nail tech scheduling app" all resolve to the same commercial SERP. They were consolidated — homepage covers the full cluster via synonym reinforcement. No `/nail-tech-booking-software` page was created; it would have been a near-duplicate. Wave 1 delivers 4 new pages instead of 5.
