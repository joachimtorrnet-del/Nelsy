## Strategic Shift from Wave 1

Wave 1 was acquisition-heavy: 2 branded comparison pages, 1 feature page, 1 Instagram-specific page. Wave 2 rebalances toward topical authority, educational depth and linkable utility tools — the content that earns organic reach, builds trust and keeps nail techs coming back.

Target mix:
| Category | % of Wave 2 | Pages |
|----------|------------|-------|
| High-intent commercial | 20% | best-booking-app roundup |
| Booking/business SEO | 30% | deposit policy, cancellation policy |
| Educational content | 30% | how to get more nail clients, pricing guide |
| Free tools | 20% | pricing calculator, cancellation policy generator |

## Cannibalization Analysis

### Conflict 1: Cancellation policy guide vs. generator
`/learn/nail-tech-cancellation-policy` + `/tools/nail-tech-cancellation-policy-generator` share the same SERP intent. Building both creates a cannibalization pair.

**Decision:** Build ONE page: `/tools/nail-tech-cancellation-policy-generator`  
Structure: interactive generator above the fold → rich explanatory content below (covers what the guide would have said: notice windows, deposit treatment, no-show fees, rescheduling rules, disclaimer). One strong page outranks two thin ones. Redirect any future `/learn/nail-tech-cancellation-policy` to the generator.

### Conflict 2: Deposit policy guide vs. Wave 1 deposits page
`/learn/nail-tech-deposit-policy` vs `/nail-tech-deposits` (Wave 1).

**Wave 1 page** = product feature page: how Nelsy automates deposit collection.  
**Wave 2 page** = business strategy guide: why to require deposits, how to write your policy, what amount to charge, refund rules, template.

These are **distinct intents** — keep both. Cross-link them. The Wave 2 page converts by linking to the Wave 1 page ("once you have a policy, Nelsy enforces it automatically").

### Conflict 3: Pricing guide vs. pricing calculator
Different formats, different intents. The guide is informational (understanding); the calculator is action-oriented (doing). They are natural companions, not competitors. Build both. Each links to the other prominently.

### Conflict 4: Instagram bio vs. Instagram booking (Wave 1)
Distinct intents. Wave 1 = booking flow from Instagram. Wave 2 = writing the bio. No conflict. Strong internal link opportunity.

## URL Structure Decision

New pages will use a `/learn/` prefix for editorial content and `/tools/` for interactive tools. This establishes clear site architecture and signals content type to Google.

| Type | Prefix | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| Feature pages | root | `/nail-tech-deposits` |
| Comparison pages | root | `/fresha-alternative-nail-techs` |
| Editorial guides | `/learn/` | `/learn/nail-tech-pricing-guide` |
| Interactive tools | `/tools/` | `/tools/nail-tech-pricing-calculator` |
